## Frozen diagnostic before new outcome
Adult documented cue = explicit positive airsac OR vocalisation OR amplexus; no recorded cue = all four source flags explicitly negative; tadpole-only separated. Unknown markers and ambiguous date formats retained as unclassified, not inferred negatives. Exact species/family join to historical Nunes 2021 data; duplicate names excluded, no genus-based extrapolation. Strict no-ground E sensitivity only codes 44,45,51–55. Compare record-weighted monthly profiles and equal-record-count within-species × climate contrasts, >=10 adult-cue and >=20 no-recorded-cue records per unit; equal-size subsampling 500 draws; weight species equally across climate units. At least five species per class for descriptive support, not confirmatory inference. No recorded cue is not evidence of no reproduction. Data not independent of GBIF citizen science and may overlap other records; no direct recipient mating or fertilization. The original V1.8 primary result remains unchanged.
