## Interpretation and next step
A camera-space conspicuity contrast between the sampled foam structures and nonfoam egg clutches is a descriptive candidate, not an estimated global evolutionary effect. Only five species; distinct clades, habitats, backgrounds, stages, camera processing and photographer selection; photos need not represent independent nests. No sheltered comparison. This does not demonstrate causal attraction, approach, avoidance, food use, mating or fertilization. No claim that the foam's appearance evolved as a signal. Preserve earlier negative/underpowered temporal results.

Next: expand taxon- and substrate-matched genuine nests/egg clutches, add bright nonreproductive structures and standardized shape/area controls, assess spatial contrast at calibrated viewing distances. Use actual 300–700 nm reflectance, illumination and receiver-specific sensitivities for visual model; RGB cannot identify UV or reconstruct spectrum. Test cue vs food-like appearance and visual-only vs chemistry with behavioral manipulations. Draft PR #10 unmerged.
