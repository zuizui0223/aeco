## Interpretation
In selected images, foam is similar in two camera-space metrics to white floral displays and pale fungi, not uniquely floral. High luminance contrast also occurs in yellow fungi. This is not formal equivalence, convergent evolution, mimicry or attraction evidence. Deliberate white/colored reference selection, sparse samples, uncontrolled lighting/exposure/stage/habitat, incomplete masks, distinct sources and lineages, no UV or spectral response. No measured animal vision or actual reproduction. Sheltered nest images remain unavailable. A more interesting next test is generic conspicuity versus food-associated color/shape: contrast-matched white and colored forms, white nonreproductive surfaces, visual vs chemical cues; distinguish detection, approach, contact and acceptance. For a macro comparison, require validated appearance from diverse taxa, matched backgrounds, size/distance and sampling, actual spectral reflectance plus receptor models rather than mapping RGB to insect UV.
