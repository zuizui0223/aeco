These are repeated group measurements, not 93 independent clutches; replacement ability persisted through the tested 4.5–18.5-day interval. This is renewal by larvae in occupied chambers, not postdeparture persistence or external insect access. Supplementary PDF screenshot failed; extracted table and article text used.

## Interpretation and next empirical gate
Distinguish parent production, offspring renewal, postdeparture decay, accessibility and recipient fertilization. A generic process model is stock(t+1)=stock(t)*survival(t)+parent_input(t)+larval_renewal(t), with accessibility distinct from stock. A spatial proxy based on remote-sensed climate/wetness alone cannot estimate these terms without actual observation. Next: quantify temperature buffering and hatch outcomes using verified Japanese raw measurements at nest/day rather than timestamp grain, then seek measured repeated surface wetness or postdeparture visible-area loss; keep external access and internal protection separate. Daily formation/decay records required before interpreting lag within a month, no arbitrary new threshold searches. No direct Pieridae foam visitation, mating or confirmed recipient fertilization established. PR #7 remains unmerged.
